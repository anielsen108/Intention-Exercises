# Remaining product work

The current app provides a complete local practice loop and retains the original deployment setup. These are the most useful next investments after the redesign:

1. **Recorded human contrasts.** Add consented, carefully directed speech examples from several speakers and accents. Each pair should use the same sentence and specify the conversational situation. Keep the existing hums for isolating pitch movement.
2. **Perceptual validation.** Ask learners and coaches whether the prompts produce audible, usable contrasts. Test whether the retry cues help more than playback alone before adding stronger scoring claims.
3. **Physical-device coverage.** Check built-in, USB, and headset microphones in Edge, Firefox, Safari, and iOS. Pay attention to Bluetooth routing, interrupted recordings, and permission changes.
4. **Corpus editing.** Review partial collections and uneven source examples. Consolidate repeated sentences into intentional contrast sets. Base counts and labels on actual entries instead of planned totals.
5. **Longer performance analysis.** Word-level feedback would require reliable alignment and separate validation of stress and pause measurements. Preserve the current distinction between pitch-shape comparison and listener interpretation.

No account, cloud audio service, or automatic emotion grading is required for the current training experience.
